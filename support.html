Common questions

Is Sis a doctor?
No. Sis gives general skincare education and builds routines from your products. She never diagnoses. For strong irritation, allergic reactions, changing moles or persistent acne, please see a dermatologist. In an emergency (swelling of the face, lips or tongue, trouble breathing), call 112.

Sis got something wrong about a product.
Open the product and tap "Report a mistake". It reaches us right away.

Why does the app ask before using AI?
Chat and product checks use an AI (Anthropic's Claude). Nothing is sent to it until you agree. You can turn it off any time in Profile → Privacy; your routine, shelf, lessons and SOS keep working.

How do I delete my data?
Profile → Delete all my data. It erases everything on your phone and your account on our server, permanently. To download a copy first: Profile → Privacy → Download a copy of my data.

How do I leave Circle or block someone?
Circle → ••• → Leave Circle deletes your Circle name, friends and nudges. To block or report a friend, open their card in Circle and tap •••.

My products disappeared after changing phones.
Your data lives on your phone and in your phone's backup. Restoring the new phone from that backup brings it back.
