Skin Sis — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Skin Sis is a skincare app made by Ana Hodoroaga ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with your information, in plain words.

Short version: your skin profile, shelf, routine, progress, chats and photos live on your phone. We only send what's needed to answer you, we never sell your data, there are no ads and no tracking, and you can delete everything in one tap.

1. What stays on your phone

  • Your answers to the skin questionnaire, including any health details you choose to share (only with your explicit consent).
  • Your shelf, routine, schedules, ingredient alerts and notes for Sis.
  • Your progress: routines ticked, streaks, Glow points, skin check-ins.
  • Your chats with Sis and any photos you take in the app.

This data is stored in the app's private storage, encrypted by iOS. It is not uploaded to our servers.

2. What is sent, and why

To answer you, some information has to leave your phone:

When you…What is sentWhere
Chat with SisYour recent messages, any photo you attach, and a summary of your skin profile, shelf and routineOur server (EU), then Anthropic's Claude AI to write the answer
Check or add a productThe product name, barcode or photo, and a summary of your skin profile and shelfOur server (EU), then Anthropic's Claude AI; public product databases (Open Beauty Facts) for the product's name and ingredients
Send feedback or report a mistakeYour message, the product (if any) and the app versionOur server (EU)
  • Anthropic processes this data only to generate the answer, under its commercial terms, which do not allow it to use the data to train its models.
  • Photos are sent only to get an answer and are not stored on our servers.
  • Public product facts that Sis finds (a product's name, ingredient list and sources, never anything about you) are saved in a shared catalog, so the next person who looks up the same product gets it faster.

3. Your account

When you first open the app, it creates an anonymous account: a random identifier, with no name, email or phone number. It's used to keep your chats secure and to count free-plan limits. Our servers are run by Supabase in the European Union (Ireland).

4. What we don't do

  • No advertising, no ad tracking, no selling or renting of your data.
  • No analytics that identify you.
  • No access to your contacts, location or photo library beyond the photos you choose.

5. Health and face photos

Skin and health details and photos of your face are sensitive data under the GDPR. We only use them with your explicit consent, which you give inside the app before sharing them, and only to personalise your skincare advice. Skin Sis is not a medical service and doesn't diagnose.

6. Your rights and deleting your data

  • Delete everything: Profile → Delete all my data. This deletes your data on your phone and your account and everything linked to it on our server, permanently.
  • You can change your answers and notes at any time in Profile.
  • Under the GDPR you can also ask us for access, correction, export or deletion, and you can complain to your data protection authority (in Romania: ANSPDCP).

7. Age

Skin Sis is for people aged 16 and over.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app when the change matters.

9. Contact

Questions about your data: in the app, go to Profile → Send feedback.
