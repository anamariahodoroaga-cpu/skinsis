Skin Sis — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Skin Sis is a skincare app. This policy explains, in plain words, what happens to your information.

Short version: your skin profile, shelf, routine, progress, chats and photos are saved on your phone. Something leaves your phone only when it's needed to answer you, and only after you agree. No ads, no tracking, no selling of data. You can download or delete everything from the app at any time.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Ana Hodoroaga, Romania, who makes Skin Sis. Contact: skinsis.app@gmail.com.

2. What is saved on your phone

  • Your questionnaire answers. Health details (pregnancy, allergies, skin conditions, medication) only if you give explicit consent.
  • Your shelf, routine choices, schedules, ingredient alerts, reminders and notes for Sis.
  • Your progress: routines ticked, streaks, Glow points, skin check-ins.
  • Your chats with Sis and the photos you take in the app.

It's stored in the app's private storage, encrypted by iOS, and isn't uploaded to our servers.

4. Why we're allowed to use it (legal bases)

  • To provide the app you asked for (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)): your account, product checks, chat, feedback.
  • Your explicit consent (art. 9(2)(a) and art. 6(1)(a)): health details, photos of your face or skin, and sending data to Sis's AI. You can withdraw consent at any time in the app; this doesn't affect what was done before.
  • Our legitimate interest (art. 6(1)(f)): keeping the service safe and fair, for example counting daily chats to prevent abuse.

5. Who processes data for us

  • Supabase (servers in the EU, Ireland): runs our server, your anonymous account, usage counters, the shared product catalog and feedback.
  • Anthropic (USA): the Claude AI that writes Sis's answers and checks products. Anthropic processes this data only to answer you and doesn't use it to train its models. Transfers outside the EU are protected by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses in Anthropic's data processing terms.
  • Open Beauty Facts (open product database): receives product names or barcodes you search for, like any website would.
  • Apple delivers the app and runs iOS. Reminders are scheduled on your phone.

We don't sell or rent your data, show ads or use tracking or advertising tools.

6. Your anonymous account

When you first open the app, it creates an account with no name, email or phone number: just a random identifier. It keeps your connection secure and counts free-plan limits.

8. Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • See and correct your data: Profile shows what Sis knows, and you can update your answers and notes.
  • Download a copy (portability): Profile → Privacy → Download a copy of my data.
  • Withdraw consent: Profile → Privacy (Sis's AI, health details, photos in chat).
  • Delete everything: Profile → Delete all my data. This erases your data on the phone and your account, with everything linked to it, on our server, permanently.
  • Object or ask for restriction, or ask us anything about your data: skinsis.app@gmail.com. We answer within one month.
  • Complain to a data protection authority. In Romania that's ANSPDCP (www.dataprotection.ro).

9. Scores and advice

Product scores and routine suggestions are automated suggestions to help with skincare. They have no legal or similarly significant effect on you, and you're always free to ignore them. Skin Sis isn't a medical service, doesn't diagnose and doesn't replace a doctor or dermatologist.

10. Security

Data on your phone is encrypted by iOS. Everything sent from the app travels encrypted (HTTPS). Our server's database is protected so each account can only reach its own data.

11. Age

Skin Sis is for people aged 16 and over.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app when the change matters.
